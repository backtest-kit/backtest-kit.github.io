ISweepTrade

Properties

ideaId

ideaId: number

Identifier of the idea that triggered the trade.

symbol

symbol: string

Trading pair of the trade — carried on the trade itself so a multi-symbol dump (score over many tickers concatenates per-symbol runs) stays grep-distinguishable: trades of different symbols never blur into one another.

author

author: string

Author of the triggering idea — carried on the trade itself so per-author analysis (score, voting, top performers) reads straight off the artifact, without joining ideaId back to the ideas feed. The whole strategy is about authors; the trade names its own.

direction

direction: SweepIdeaDirection

Position direction inherited from the idea.

entryTimestamp

entryTimestamp: number

Unix timestamp in milliseconds of the trade entry minute.

exitTimestamp

exitTimestamp: number

Unix timestamp in milliseconds of the exit candle.

exitReason

exitReason: SweepExitReason

Why the trade was closed.

holdMinutesActual

holdMinutesActual: number

Actual holding time, minutes (entry candle inclusive).

pnlPercent

pnlPercent: number

Trade PnL percent, net of fees on both legs.

absorbedIdeas

absorbedIdeas: ISweepAbsorbedIdea[]

Ideas that qualified for entry but were ABSORBED by this trade holding the slot — each {ideaId, author}, so which author's signals a long hold ate is visible idea by idea, no feed join.
